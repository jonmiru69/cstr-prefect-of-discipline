Prefect of Discipline
Privacy notice

This portal supports confidential school incident recording, review, follow-up, and documented disciplinary decisions for Grades 7–12.

Information handled

Records may include student names and school IDs, age, grade, section, strand, incident details, statements, meeting notes, findings, actions, dates, and the staff members responsible for changes. The school should collect only information needed for the case.

Access and use

School-approved personnel use Google sign-in, a personal school invitation, and an authenticator to access the portal. Approved users may view Junior and Senior High School records; their assigned role controls changes. Record views, searches, exports, and changes are logged for accountability. Exported documents remain confidential.

Service providers

The website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Supabase provides the database and authentication service. Google provides account sign-in. Google sign-in requests basic identity information only; it does not request Gmail messages or Google Drive access. Fonts are served with this website. Student records are not stored in the GitHub source repository.

Retention and requests

CSTR is responsible for determining the purpose and lawful basis for processing, the applicable retention schedule, authorized disclosures, and responses to privacy requests. Contact the school office or its designated Data Protection Officer to request the school’s current privacy policy, ask about a record, request a correction, or raise a privacy concern. Records are not automatically erased by this application.

Your device

Sign-in information is held in this browser tab’s session storage. Sign out when finished and protect downloaded files. Do not share an account, invitation code, authenticator key, or confidential document.

This notice describes this portal. The school’s approved privacy policy and procedures apply to its use. Ask the school office for the official contact details and retention information.
